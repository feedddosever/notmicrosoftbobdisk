# Data sources

## Synthetic data

All workbook, manual, policy and rate data in this repository is **synthetic**.

- The carrier, **Example Mutual Insurance Co. (FICTIONAL)**, is invented. Every rate,
  factor, rule, territory and fee was made up for a software demonstration.
- Policies carry generated IDs only (`HO-000001`, ...). There are no names, addresses,
  contacts or other personal data.
- The workbook is generated by `tools/gen_workbook.py --n 40 --seed 2026`; test inputs
  are generated by the seeded harness generator.
- The three spreadsheet anomalies in the workbook were seeded deliberately.
