## Governing rule — verbatim

**R-510 Fees and tax** (p. 7):

> "Premium tax (¢) = territory tax rate (R-900) × (written premium + policy fee)."
>
> "There are no per-policy overrides of tax: the premium tax is always calculated by this rule and is never entered or adjusted by hand for an individual policy."

---

## Discrepancy

Cell `Calc!AM17` contains the literal value `48.17` instead of the formula `=ROUND((AJ17+AK17)*G17,2)`.

**Computed value by the column rule:** The service calculates `tax` = `ROUND((premium_rounded + policy_fee) × tax_rate, 2)`.  
For policy HO-000016 (zone T03, tax rate 2%, premium_rounded = 1301, policy_fee = 25):  
`ROUND((1301 + 25) × 0.02, 2)` = `ROUND(26.52, 2)` = **$26.52**

**Workbook hard-coded value:** **$48.17**  
**Delta:** workbook overstates tax by **$21.65** (total_due is inflated by $21.65 for this policy).

The manual explicitly prohibits hand entry: *"the premium tax is always calculated by this rule and is never entered or adjusted by hand for an individual policy."*

---

## Analysis of options

### `adopt-manual`
Replace the hard-coded value with the formula `=ROUND((AJ17+AK17)*G17,2)` (or its equivalent in the service). This is required by the explicit prohibition in R-510. The service result of $26.52 is the correct filed premium tax.

### `escalate`
Appropriate only if there is a documented regulatory or accounting reason for the $48.17 figure — for example, a prior correction entry or a surcharge not otherwise in the manual. The manual's explicit prohibition makes this unlikely to be sanctioned, but escalation is warranted if the origin of the hard-coded value is unknown and needs investigation.

---

## Recommendation

**`adopt-manual`** — R-510 contains an unambiguous blanket prohibition on per-policy hand adjustments to premium tax. The computed tax is $26.52; the workbook shows $48.17, inflating the total due by $21.65 for policy HO-000016. There is no manual authority for this override. The service implementation correctly applies the formula.

**Confidence: High** — The manual text leaves no interpretive room ("never entered or adjusted by hand for an individual policy"). The premium delta is confirmed at −$21.65 across the single affected row.
