# D-003 — Anomaly Brief

> **Note:** This anomaly was seeded deliberately for the SheetShift software demonstration.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Anomaly ID** | D-003 / Lint A3 |
| **Cells** | `Calc!X31` (single cell, `credit_pct` output, policy HO-000030) |
| **Workbook formula** | `=W31` |
| **Column rule** | `=MIN(W31,CreditCap)` |
| **Manual rule** | R-310, p. 5 (see verbatim quote below) |
| **Rows affected** | 1 row (row 31, policy HO-000030) |
| **Premium impact** | min +$255.77 · median +$255.77 · max +$255.77 · rows changed 1 |
| **Options allowed** | `adopt-manual` · `escalate` |
| **Recommendation** | `adopt-manual` (see reasoning below) |
| **Confidence** | High |

---

## Governing rule — verbatim

**R-310 Premium credits** (p. 5):

> "A policy earns each credit below whose condition it meets. Credits are added together, and the total credits shall not exceed 25%. The net AOP premium is the AOP premium × (1 - total credits), rounded to the cent. Credits apply to the AOP premium only, not to the hurricane premium."

**Table 310** (p. 5):

| Credit | Percentage | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Central alarm | 5% | The alarm indicator is Y. |
| Claims-free | 10% | See R-320. |
| New home | 8% | Home age is 5 years or less. |
| Wind mitigation, basic | 4% | Wind mitigation is Basic. |
| Wind mitigation, fortified | 10% | Wind mitigation is Fortified. |

---

## Discrepancy

Cell `Calc!X31` contains the formula `=W31` (the raw credit sum with no cap) instead of `=MIN(W31,CreditCap)` (the raw sum capped at 25%).

**Effect:** For policy HO-000030 (alarm=Y, claims_3yr=0, wind_mit=Fortified, year_built=2023, effective_date=2027-06-01), the raw credits are:  
- Central alarm: 5%  
- Wind mitigation, Fortified: 10%  
- New home (home_age ≤ 5, born 2023, effective 2027): 8%  
- Claims-free (claims=0, home_age ≥ 3): 10%  

**Raw total `credit_raw` (W31) = 5% + 10% + 8% + 10% = 33%**

The manual caps total credits at 25% (`CreditCap`). The column rule `=MIN(W31,CreditCap)` correctly returns **0.25**.  
The anomalous formula `=W31` returns **0.33**, applying an 8 percentage-point excess credit.

This causes the net AOP premium to be under-rated for this policy and cascades to all downstream outputs (aop_net, subtotal, term_premium, premium_rounded, assessment, tax, total_due — 10 cells mismatch as reported in the runtime group G01). The workbook's total_due is **$255.77 lower** than the service value, meaning the workbook under-charges this policy.

---

## Analysis of options

### `adopt-manual`
Use `=MIN(W31,CreditCap)` (i.e., cap `credit_pct` at 25% as R-310 requires). The service already implements this correctly. This eliminates the $255.77 undercharge for policy HO-000030.

### `escalate`
Appropriate if there is a question about whether the cap applies to the full combination of credits in this scenario, or if a regulatory filing amendment removed the cap for specific credit combinations. However, R-310 is unambiguous and does not carve out exceptions.

---

## Recommendation

**`adopt-manual`** — R-310 states plainly that "total credits shall not exceed 25%." The workbook cell `=W31` omits the `MIN(..., CreditCap)` guard, allowing a 33% credit where only 25% is permitted. The service correctly applies the cap. Adopting the manual removes the $255.77 undercharge (total_due delta +$255.77, i.e., workbook charges less than the filed rate).

**Confidence: High** — The manual cap language is explicit and unqualified. The affected cell is a single isolated formula exception in an otherwise uniformly-formulaed column. The runtime delta is confirmed at +$255.77 across the single affected row.
